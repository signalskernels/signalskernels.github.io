_Last updated_: August 22, 2026

You can have your **Nucleus Islam** account and all data associated with it permanently deleted at any time.

## How to request deletion

Email **[support@signalskernels.com](mailto:support@signalskernels.com?subject=Delete%20my%20Nucleus%20Islam%20account)** from the email address the account was created with, with the subject line "Delete my Nucleus Islam account." Writing from the account's own address is how we verify the request; no other proof is needed.

## What is deleted

- Your account credentials (email, display name) from our authentication provider.
- All learning progress stored under your account — completed stations and checkpoint records.
- Any support correspondence associated with the request, after it is resolved.

Deletion is completed within **30 days** and confirmed by reply. It is permanent: your progress cannot be restored afterwards.

## What is not on our servers

Private lesson notes and bookmarks are stored only on your device and are removed by uninstalling the app — they never reach us.

## Partial deletion

If you want your learning progress reset but the account kept, say so in the email and we will clear the progress only.

The full picture of what the app stores and why is in the [Nucleus Islam Privacy Policy](/nucleus-islam-privacy/).
