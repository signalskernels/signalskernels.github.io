_Last updated_: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Lahu Qalb** (له قلب) mobile application ("Lahu Qalb," "the App," "we," "us," "our") handles information. Lahu Qalb is published by **Signals Kernels AI LLC** ("the Company").

Lahu Qalb is a set of narrated, animated Arabic picture books for young children. Everything the App needs (the stories, pictures, narration and sounds) is inside the App when it is installed. **The App does not connect to the internet**, and it does not collect any information about your child or about you.

## The Short Version

- **We collect nothing.** The App has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools.
- **The App cannot go online.** It does not have Android's internet permission, so the App itself cannot send anything off the phone.
- **Reading progress stays on the phone.** The App remembers which "What would you do?" cards your child has finished, so it can mark them. Uninstalling the App deletes it from the phone. If your phone backs up its apps to your Google account, that backup may include this list (see Section 1).
- **There are no links, purchases or sharing** inside the App.

## 1. Information Stored On Your Device

The App stores one kind of information, **only on your device**: for each story, a list of the after-story cards your child has finished, such as the "What would you do if…?" moments.

- **What it contains:** short internal card identifiers. It contains no name, age, voice, picture, location or anything else about your child.
- **Where it is kept:** the App's private storage on the phone. Other apps cannot read it.
- **Who receives it:** no one. The App never sends it to us or to anyone else.
- **Your phone's backup:** if Android's backup to your Google account is turned on, Android may include this list in that backup, as it does for most apps, and put it back if you reinstall the App. The backup is kept in your own Google account under Google's terms. We cannot see it.
- **How to delete it:** uninstall the App, or clear the App's storage in your phone's settings. To delete a backed-up copy as well, manage your backups in your Google account.

## 2. Information Sent Off Your Device

**None.** The App does not send any information off your device. It has no server, and it does not use any third-party service while it runs.

If you write to us by email, we receive whatever you choose to send, including the address you write from. We use it only to reply to you.

## 3. What We Do Not Do

- We **do not** collect personal information from children or from parents.
- We **do not** show ads, and we do not use the Advertising ID.
- We **do not** use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
- We **do not** sell, share or rent any information, because we have none.
- We **do not** let children make purchases, chat, share anything or open websites from the App.

## 4. Permissions

The App asks for **no permissions** while it runs: there is no prompt for the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or notifications.

The App's Android package includes two permissions that come with the software libraries it uses. Neither asks you anything, and neither reads personal information:

- **"View network connections"** (`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`) comes with the audio-player library that plays the narration, which can check whether a network is available. Because the App has no internet permission, nothing is sent.
- **An internal permission** (`DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION`) comes with Android's support library. It is private to the App, and it stops other apps from sending the App system messages.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Lahu Qalb is made for children, and it is designed so that no child's personal information is collected at all. It is designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR's rules for children's data and the Google Play Families Policy by collecting no personal information.

If you believe the App has somehow collected information about your child, contact us at the address below. We will investigate and delete anything under our control.

## 6. Google Play

The App is distributed through **Google Play**, which is operated by Google LLC under its own terms and [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google may share aggregated statistics with us, such as install counts, and crash reports from devices whose owners chose to share them with app developers. That information comes from Google, not from the App. It does not identify your child.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or similar laws. These include the right to access, correct or delete personal information. We hold no personal information from the App. The reading progress on your device can be deleted at any time, as described in Section 1. For anything else, such as an email you sent us, contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

If the App's handling of information ever changes, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date before the new version of the App is released.
